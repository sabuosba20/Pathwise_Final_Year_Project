Identity study 02

Pathwise

Five scalable marks for personalised learning direction. Every concept is a real, editable SVG and uses the existing Pathwise terracotta.

01

Route P

Letter abstraction · bold

A continuous P becomes a route, with its lower endpoint marking the student’s starting position.

02

Path Aperture

Negative space · iconic

A route is carved from a stable circle. The detached destination point creates tension and forward pull.

03

Forward Fold

Geometry · energetic

A compact P-like loop overlaps a forward chevron: personalised profile meeting decisive movement.

04

Learning Arc

Capsule matrix · friendly

Course modules gather into a rising arc, ending in one confident goal. Modular, warm and easy to animate.

05

Guided Branch

Node network · personalised

Several possible directions exist, but one path is intentionally stronger—the recommendation chosen for you.
